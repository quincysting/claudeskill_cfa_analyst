We're an LP in a $100m private equity fund. All $100m was called at the start (t = 0) and split equally between two deals. Deal A was sold at the end of year 2 for $100m; Deal B was sold at the end of year 5 for $30m. The LPA has an 8% compound preferred return accruing on unreturned contributed capital plus unpaid preferred return, a 100% GP catch-up, and 20% carried interest. (1) Under a deal-by-deal (American) waterfall, how much carry would the GP have received when Deal A was sold, and how much would they have to give back under a clawback that limits total carry to what a whole-of-fund (European) waterfall pays? (2) Under the whole-of-fund (European) waterfall, how much does each party get and when, what is the GP's total carry, and what are the LPs' net IRR and net multiple?
