Volatility is 28%, the continuously compounded risk-free rate is 4.5%, and XYZ has a 1.5% continuous dividend yield. Price a 6-month European put struck at $110 with Black-Scholes-Merton, find the call strike that makes a zero-cost collar, show what the position is worth at expiry if XYZ is at $90, $120 and $150, and walk me through the trade-offs and anything I should consider for this client.
